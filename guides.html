Frequently asked questions

Is MozTools free?

Yes. The free plan has honest limits: PDF → Word supports up to 2 pages / 3 MB, and the first request after inactivity may take up to 60 seconds while the server wakes up.

Why is PDF → Word limited to 2 pages?

The conversion runs on free hosting with limited memory. Larger PDFs are refused with a clear message instead of crashing the server, so every visitor sees predictable behaviour.

What is a text-based PDF?

One where you can click and drag to highlight the text. Scanned PDFs (photos of printed pages) may not convert well and would need OCR, which is not part of the free engine.

Will my file be stored?

No. Files are deleted from the server immediately after the converted file is returned to you.

Why does the first conversion take longer?

The free server sleeps after inactivity and takes up to 60 seconds to wake up. Later conversions on the same visit are fast.

Why File Conversion Matters

In today’s digital world, we deal with dozens of different file formats every single day. From Microsoft Word documents and Excel spreadsheets to image files and PDFs, the sheer variety of formats can be overwhelming. This is where file conversion becomes absolutely essential. File conversion is the process of taking a document, presentation, or image and translating it into a different format so that it can be opened, viewed, or edited by different software.

At MozTools, we believe that converting files should be fast, free, and accessible to everyone without the need to download heavy software or create an account. Whether you are a student submitting an assignment, a professional sending a business proposal, or someone just trying to organize their digital photos, our suite of tools is designed to make your life easier.

Understanding the Formats

Different formats serve different purposes. For example, Microsoft Word (DOCX) is designed for editing text. It allows you to change fonts, adjust margins, and track changes. However, if you send a DOCX file to someone who does not have Microsoft Word, the formatting might break completely. That is why converting to PDF (Portable Document Format) is so important. A PDF locks your document in place, ensuring it looks exactly the same on a phone, a tablet, or a computer, no matter what software the recipient uses.

Similarly, spreadsheets created in Excel (XLSX) are fantastic for calculations and data analysis. But when it comes time to share a financial report with a client, an Excel to PDF conversion is the most professional choice. It prevents the recipient from accidentally altering your formulas or data.

The Power of PDFs

PDFs are the universal standard for sharing documents. But what if you receive a PDF and need to edit the text inside it? That is where our PDF to Word (DOCX) tool comes in. By using advanced extraction technology, we can help you convert text-based PDFs back into editable Word documents. While our free engine has a limit of 2 pages / 3 MB for this specific feature to ensure server stability, it is a powerful tool for recovering text quickly.

We also offer a Merge PDF tool, which is incredibly useful for combining multiple documents into one organized file. If you have a separate cover letter, resume, and portfolio, merging them into a single PDF ensures the recruiter reads everything in the exact order you intended.
